§02 — The story

The world's shortest billionaire career.

Ten million people watched a British YouTuber become richer than Elon Musk. Five chapters. Roughly seven minutes of wealth.

Keep scrolling

01 The plan

Ten billion shares. Sell one.

Register a company with 10,000,000,000 shares. Sell just one for £50. Then argue the whole thing is worth £500 billion — enough, on paper, to beat the world's richest.

02 The company

Registration form · Nature of business

SIC 10730 — Manufacture of macaroni, noodles, couscous and similar farinaceous products

Unlimited Money Limited.

It didn't actually do anything. When the form demanded a business activity, he ticked the manufacture of macaroni, noodles and couscous. Knowing the terminology was optional.

03 The investor
SOLD1 of 10B

A suit, a table, and Hope.

He set up in London's financial district and pitched strangers. Most said no. Then a woman named Hope bought one share for £50 — she had a feeling something might come of it.

04 The letter

£500,000,000,000. In writing.

Two weeks later a valuation adviser put the implied value at £500 billion. The same letter warned that with no revenue, the setup risked accusations of fraud.

05 The fall
Dissolved

Dissolved. Richest for ~7 minutes.

Advised to shut it down, he dissolved the company. The £500 billion never reached a bank account. It just proved how absurd numbers get without a real business behind them.
